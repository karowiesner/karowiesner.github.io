Complex systems are studied in biology, economics, neuroscience, climate science and many other fields. This book asks what they have in common. It looks for the shared conceptual and mathematical foundations of complexity science, reviews the main features of complex systems and the different ways of defining and measuring complexity, and sets out the authors' own framework.
